What you type is saved automatically in this browser as you go, so you may close the window and continue later from the same browser. Autosave alone does not send your response. Used only to tell responses apart. Initials or a field of work are enough; a full name is not needed.

What you are judging. In this task the substructure a molecule must contain is stated as a natural-language sentence. What you are checking is whether that natural-language structural constraint describes the given substructure well.

How. Compare the substructure below (SMARTS and drawing) with the natural-language description, then answer the questions underneath. You do not need to draw or design any structure yourself.

Reference material. You may open the collapsed sections if they help. Neither is what you are judging.

Scope. The structural constraint is given for a substructure, not for the whole molecule. Please judge everything against the given substructure alone. Anything that appears only in the example final molecule, and not in the substructure, is not a gap if the description leaves it out.
