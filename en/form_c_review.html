What to do. Please read the description of our framework below and answer the two questions at the end. The Review step described in the middle is what this form is checking.

Framework overview. SREV-Mol takes a structural constraint written in natural language and a set of property constraints as its input, and generates a molecule that satisfies both at once.

Recommending edit candidates (suggest_edits). This is the step where the molecule already satisfies the structural constraint but some of the property constraints are still out of range, and the molecule is changed a little at a time to bring the remaining properties inside their ranges. The edits come from a database aggregated over pairs of molecules that share a common part and differ only in the transformed part. For every transformation it stores, per property, the mean shift (δ) and the standard deviation (std). From that database the agent is given four edits that would not break the structural constraint the current molecule has to keep while moving it closer to the property constraints, and each candidate comes back with the mean shift (δ) and standard deviation (std) for the constrained properties. These numbers are statistics over existing data, not settled outcomes. So at every step the agent has to judge which edit suits this particular molecule best and pick it.

That judgement is the Review. The LLM goes through a fixed-format reasoning it calls Review and selects one of the four candidates. This form asks you, as an expert, to confirm whether the format and the content of that reasoning are effective in the actual editing process and chemically sound. We are not asking whether this one case got the right answer; the format repeats identically at every selection step, so the single case below is enough to go on.

Terms used in the reasoning
holdsthe property stays inside its constraint even allowing for the uncertainty of the predicted shift
at riskthe predicted value itself is inside the constraint, but allowing for the uncertainty it could fall outside
within reachthe predicted value is outside the constraint, but the uncertainty range reaches it
missesit falls short of the constraint even allowing for the uncertainty
addsthe functional groups and rings that edit creates (new here = not present anywhere else in the molecule either)
dropsthe functional groups and rings that edit removes
+N carbon …the change in atom composition across the edit (adds no atoms = no change in atom count)
